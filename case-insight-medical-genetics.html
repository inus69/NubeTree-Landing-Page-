Case study · USA

Cryptographic, HIPAA-Compliant Data Structures

Client
Insight Medical Genetics
Region
USA
Domain
HIPAA / Salesforce Shield

The problem

Sensitive genetic sequencing records and diagnostic logs put the client under federal regulatory scrutiny. The platform had to protect that data without slowing clinical reporting.

The work

  • Deployed Salesforce Shield so encryption keys protect sensitive standard and custom text fields, including patient name, SSN, and genetic marker fields, at the database layer.
  • Structured profiles, permission sets, and restriction rules so genetic data stays isolated and only authorized medical personnel can view unmasked clinical values.
  • Activated Shield Event Monitoring to stream setup logs, API usage, and interface activity into a SIEM tool, with alerts for unusual export activity.

The outcome

The work met federal HIPAA audit criteria at 100%, protecting patient identity without slowing daily diagnostic reporting.
