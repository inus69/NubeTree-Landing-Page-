Case study · USA

Custom Academic Lifecycle Middleware

Client
The Washington Center
Region
USA
Domain
Academic lifecycle / Salesforce

The problem

Student enrollment, university partner verification, and housing placement software operated in separate systems. Administrators re-entered data by hand, student tracking had a high error rate, and applicant onboarding was delayed.

The work

  • Implemented webhook endpoints in Salesforce to capture third-party application submissions in real time.
  • Built Apex assignment matrices that qualify, deduplicate, and route applicant records using university affiliation rules and institutional caps.
  • Consolidated student, housing, and corporate partner data into one Lightning Console app with guided screen flows for daily program actions.

The outcome

Automated 90% of the student application tracking steps that had been manual. Average lead-to-enrollment processing fell from several days to minutes.
